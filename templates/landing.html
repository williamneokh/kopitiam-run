How to add to your Home Screen

For iPhone/iPad (Safari):

  1. First, go to the new site.
  2. Tap the 'Share' button ().
  3. Scroll down and tap 'Add to Home Screen'.

For Android (Chrome):

  1. First, go to the new site.
  2. Tap the 'More' button (three dots ).
  3. Tap 'Install app' or 'Add to Home screen'.

For other devices/browsers:

Look for a "Bookmark" or "Add to Home Screen" option in your browser's menu.
